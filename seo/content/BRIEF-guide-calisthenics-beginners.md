## Search intent

Informational → practical plan. Searcher wants a **clear beginner progression** they can do at home/park/garage with little or no equipment, ideally something printable (PDF). Secondary intent: start logging workouts without another subscription.

**Success = funnel to I-Day** (open free logger → optional Coach from logs), **not** vanity traffic or time-on-page theater.

## AI Overview risk

High for “workout plan for beginners” style queries. Expect Google AI Overview / similar to summarize generic progressions.

**Mitigations (do not overclaim):**

- Ship a **specific, usable plan** (sets/reps/rest, 3–4 day split, progression rules) — not a fluff listicle.
- Unique Mission wedge: **track the plan free, offline, no account** + optional Coach from logs (no wearable).
- PDF download / print stylesheet as differentiator for the PDF query — Craft asset.
- Structured sections + FAQ schema if Craft supports; still assume AI Overview may steal clicks.
- Internal links to `/exercises/*` for form cues beat thin “top 10 apps” filler.

## Outline (H1–H2)

**H1:** Calisthenics Workout Plan for Beginners (Home, Park, or Garage)

**Intro (≤120 words):** Who this is for (train-anywhere, minimal kit, subscription-fatigued). What you’ll get (4-week starter plan + how to progress). Soft mention: log sessions free in Mission — no account. No wearable required for Coach later.

**H2:** What you need (and what you don’t)  
Space, optional pull-up bar / rings / backpack, no gym membership. Explicit: no wearable required.

**H2:** Beginner standards (honest baselines)  
Push-up / squat / hang / row regressions. Link spoke exercise pages under `/exercises/*`.

**H2:** The 4-week beginner calisthenics plan  
Week-by-week tables (Day A/B or 3-day). Sets, reps, rest, RPE-simple language. Progression rule: when to add reps vs harder variation.

**H2:** Park / outdoor variant  
Same plan mapped to park equipment (bars, benches). Keyword support for park workout — keep secondary.

**H2:** How to log the plan (free, no account)  
CTA block → `/welcome` — offline workout logger, free forever. Soft Coach: when you want a plan from your logs, Mission Coach — no wearable.

**H2:** Free PDF / print the plan  
PDF angle for “calisthenics workout plan pdf”. Craft ships downloadable or print-CSS. Do not gate PDF behind email if that contradicts no-account wedge — prefer direct download or print.

**H2:** Common mistakes  
Ego progressions, skipping pulls, no rest days, buying another AI coach sub before logging a single week.

**H2:** FAQ  
Beginner frequency, equipment swaps, “do I need an app?”, “Coach vs wearable apps”.

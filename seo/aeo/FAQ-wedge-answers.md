## Can I log workouts in Mission Winning without an account or Wi-Fi?

Yes. The logger is free forever and works with no account. Log sets on your phone offline — park, garage, hotel gym — then sync later only if you sign in. Logging does not wait on cloud or a paid plan. Start with one real set; the rest of the app can wait.

---

## How does Mission Coach decide this week's sessions?

Mission Coach builds a fatigue-aware week from the sets you already logged. The why-this-week line cites those logs — recent load, strain, and consistency — so you see why this session exists. It is a plan from your history, not a generic template and not a social feed. Open Today after you log to read that cue.

---

## Can I see last time's numbers while I log a set?

On the set row you can compare vs-last: the previous set you logged for that lift, so load and reps stay in thumb reach. It is your own last session on the row, not a leaderboard. Use it to progress one variable at a time without leaving the workout.

---

## Do I need a wearable for Train or Mission Coach?

No wearable is required. Train logging and Mission Coach both run from the sets you enter on your phone. A watch or band can stay optional later; it is never the gate to a Coach week. Phone-only is the default path for first sessions.

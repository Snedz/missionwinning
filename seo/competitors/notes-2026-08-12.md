## Landscape (positioning, not market sizing)

### Alpha Progression — closest Coach twin

- Strong overlap on **strength coaching / progression from training data** angle.
- SEO implication: do not ignore; optional future `/compare/alpha-progression` after core Hevy/Strong/Fitbod stories.
- Differentiation for Mission: free forever **logger** with **no account**, **offline**, Coach from **logs**, **no wearable**. Stay factual; no smear.

### Strong — logger UX benchmark

- Gym-logger standard; review/alternative queries exist (**strong app review** — 170 vol, July OpenSEO).
- SEO: fair review page + “who should stay” section builds trust; Mission as optional path for subscription-fatigued / train-anywhere / no-account seekers.
- Do not claim we beat Strong on every UX dimension.

### Hevy — logger + social UX

- **hevy vs strong** — 390 vol / KD0 (July OpenSEO). Classic comparison SERP.
- SEO: win by being the **useful third option** on that SERP (neutral table + Mission wedge), not by attacking either.
- Social-feed-first is fine for them; Mission leads train-anywhere logging + Coach-from-logs.

### Fitbod — AI coach paywalled

- **fitbod alternative** — 110 vol / KD0 (July OpenSEO).
- SEO: speak to paywall / subscription fatigue; contrast Coach from logs vs closed AI programming. No wearable requirement on Mission side.
- Acknowledge Fitbod convenience for people happy to pay.

### Freeletics — bodyweight brand + programs

- Relevant to calisthenics / bodyweight ICP overlap.
- SEO: secondary; optional alternative page later. Guide + exercises clusters may capture intent before compare.

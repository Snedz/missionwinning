# Loop director — air-squat (only after still PASS)

```
FORMAT
6s · 1 continuous shot · 1:1 · silent · loopable · Mission Winning Form Index

ACTIVE REFERENCES
Input still is the ONLY visual source. Animate that exact person, clothes, framing.
100% identity match. Do not invent equipment, people, or rooms.

CAMERA LOCK
Tripod locked. Zero pan, zero tilt, zero zoom, zero reframe, zero cut, zero Dutch.
What the camera is NOT doing: moving, drifting, handheld, orbit, push-in.

ACTION TIMING
0.0–1.0s: hold standing setup (or hold the still pose if already bottom)
1.0–2.5s: descend into air squat — hips back and down, hip crease below knee, arms forward
2.5–4.5s: drive up to stand, hips and knees extend together
4.5–5.5s: brief stand
5.5–6.0s: begin slight descend to loop seamlessly if still was bottom, else settle stand

PHYSICS
Bodyweight only. Feet stay planted. No morph to a different exercise. Head always fully in frame.

AUDIO
Silent.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One clean air squat · same athlete as still · head always in frame · loopable

NEGATIVES
Bar, kettlebell, camera move, cropped head, second person, text, logos, drama speed-ramps.
```

# Still director — pattern-core

```
FORMAT
1 shot · still · 1:1 · instructional Form Index · Mission Winning

SCENE CONTEXT
Clinical movement standard photograph of a FOREARM PLANK (core pattern) for long-tail exercises.
Empty paper-neutral studio — seamless floor and wall #f3f2f2. Single athlete only.

SUBJECT
One adult athlete, complete anatomy: head with neck fully inside frame.
Matte dark long sleeve and shorts or leggings. Face calm profile — fully visible, NOT cropped.

POSE / EXERCISE LOCK
- Rigid forearm plank: elbows under shoulders, forearms on floor
- Body straight line head to heels — no sag, no pike
- Feet together or hip-width, toes down
- Neutral neck, eyes toward floor slightly ahead
- This is NOT a curl, NOT a side plank, NOT a push-up

EQUIPMENT PHYSICS
Bodyweight only. Contact shadows under forearms, elbows, toes.

OPTICS
~47–50° normal lens · 4–5 m back · full body · subject 55–65% of frame.

CAMERA LOCK
True side profile · tripod locked · eye-level with mid-torso · static.

MARGINS
≥8% headroom · ≥5% foot room · full hands and feet in frame.

LIGHTING
Even soft ~5500K. No neon.

STYLE
Photoreal clinical instruction. No text/logos.

POSITIVE LOCKS
- Correct pattern exercise: forearm plank
- Full body with margins
- Side view

NEGATIVES
Bicep curl, side plank, push-up only crop, cropped head, headless, dumbbells,
hyper-tight crop, text, logos, Dutch, neon.
```

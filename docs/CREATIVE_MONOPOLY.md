# Creative monopoly filter (Zero to One → Mission Winning)

**Status: ACTIVE (2026-08-09).** A strategy *filter* on existing gates — not a second roadmap.  
**Spine wins:** product truth → [THESIS.md](THESIS.md) · what may be built → [../ORCHESTRATION.md](../ORCHESTRATION.md) · status → [../CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) `## Now` · launch → [LAUNCH_RUNBOOK.md](LAUNCH_RUNBOOK.md).

Peter Thiel’s Zero to One (via the “creative monopoly” playbook): the most valuable companies avoid competition by owning something only they can do. This file maps that playbook onto MW **honestly**. It does not claim we have a moat yet.

> [THESIS.md](THESIS.md) §5: **There is no defensible moat yet.** Shipping velocity, free-core trust, and log-derived load are assets — not a monopoly.

---

## One sentence

**Build the only free, log-native coaching loop train-anywhere athletes cannot replace with a logger + a strap + a PDF — then endure long enough that history is worth keeping.**

Pitch the **wedge**, never the north-star “everything app” as the company line.

---

## 1. Four monopoly traits (score honestly)

| Trait | MW now | Invest next |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Proprietary tech** | Log-derived load (`src/lib/coach/load.ts`), deterministic plan/adapt, anti-fabrication tests, offline outbox | Make load + earned adapt *felt* on phone; founder-signed second-beat copy ([THESIS](THESIS.md) §8) |
| **Network effects** | Near zero | **Data network of one** first (longer logs → better coach for *you*); cohort invites later — never a feed that corrupts planning ([IDENTITY_SOCIAL_PLAN](IDENTITY_SOCIAL_PLAN.md)) |
| **Economies of scale** | Software marginal cost ≈ 0; PWA | Real only after users: same engine 10 → 10k without linear founder time |
| **Strong brand** | Distinct field-manual register; free forever | Founder voice + proof free stayed free; one sentence the ICP repeats |

**Stack order for this ICP:** tech felt → brand channel → scale → network. Do not buy social graph theater before beachhead humans exist.

## 2. What valuable company is nobody building?

Not “beat Hevy.” Own the stack nobody fully owns:

> Free offline logger forever + weekly plan that adapts from **logs alone** + honest training-load reading (no strap) + re-entry without shame + phone/park ICP.

Parts exist elsewhere; **the combination** is the monopoly candidate. Anti-answer: “#1 health everything app” as the pitch.

## 4. No close substitute (product bar)

If a competitor could replace us tomorrow with Strong + PDF + WHOOP, we have not earned the monopoly. Substitute fails when:

- One-thumb outdoor log + never-drop outbox + free forever  
- Victory exits into Coach, not wallpaper  
- Coach adapt shows **why** from logs; load silent until evidence  
- Missed week → smaller session, no shame (`src/lib/reentry.ts`)  
- Em-dash / `null` under 14 days — never fabricated readiness  

**Horizon W** (founder Pass on phone): [ORCHESTRATION.md](../ORCHESTRATION.md) excellence criteria 1–5.

Three architectures, and the landing they fuse into

01–03 share nothing — no DOM, no stylesheet, no copy. That was the point: the previous board shared all three and produced eight versions of one wireframe. 04 is the continuous-scroll landing for a first-time visitor, restyled into one deepened-modernist system. The homepage stays the A1 modernist direction. The build still refuses to write if any two rows of the table match.

01

The Week

The product is the page. Log a set in the first screen and watch the week answer. Scrolling reveals consequences, not sections.

 02

Anywhere

One session, five places, one pinned stage. The ground changes under you; the plan in the corner does not.

 03

Field Manual

A document, not a scroll. Opens on a contents page. You look things up, and you may never reach the bottom.

 04

Combined landing

The fusion, in this order: pinned arrival, then the live week, then the manual, then the invite. One type system. Real links.

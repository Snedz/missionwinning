# Outreach VA brief — beta / launch week

**Purpose:** Hire a part-time contractor (10–20h) to multiply DMs and follow-ups.  
**Not:** Paid ads, fake engagement, waitlist farms, or posting as the founder without approval.  
**Scripts:** [BETA_INVITE.md](../BETA_INVITE.md) · [SOCIAL_LAUNCH.md](SOCIAL_LAUNCH.md) · Wedge: [YC_THESIS.md](YC_THESIS.md)  
**Capital policy:** [PRELAUNCH_CAPITAL.md](PRELAUNCH_CAPITAL.md) — zero paid ads until week-4 retention.

---

## Role

| Does | Does not |
|------|----------|
| Build a contact list (warm intros, Reddit/Discord usernames founder already uses, athlete friends-of-friends) | Buy email lists or bot accounts |
| Send personal DMs / emails using founder-approved templates | Claim “thousands of users” or invent metrics |
| Track replies in a shared sheet (name, channel, invited?, I-Day?, workout?) | Run Meta/TikTok/Google ads |
| Nudge day-2 / day-7 non-responders (copy from BETA_INVITE) | Post daily as Mission Winning without founder review |
| Escalate confused testers to founder within 24h | Offer refunds / legal / Stripe support |

---

## Wedge line (every message)

Free offline logger + adaptive Mission Coach from your logs (no wearable). Not “everything app.”

---

## Week-1 targets (founder sets numbers)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Outreach sends | ≥20 (prefer 40 if list exists) |
| Activated (I-Day + ≥1 workout) | ≥10 before public flip |
| Hours | Cap 10–20h this sprint |
| Paid ads spend | **$0** |

---

## Message templates

Use [BETA_INVITE.md](../BETA_INVITE.md) email + day-2/day-7 scripts. Short DM variant:

```
Hey [Name] — building Mission Winning: free offline workout logger + AI coach that adapts from your logs (no wearable / no account to start).

Looking for honest beta testers. 10 min: open link → I-Day → log one set → tell me where you got stuck.

[invite link]
```

Access codes / invite links: founder provides from Profile → Beta funnel (never commit secrets to git).

## Shared tracker columns

`name | channel | link_sent_at | replied | landed | i_day | workout | notes | day2_sent | day7_sent`

Founder reviews sheet daily; VA does not invent funnel numbers for accelerator apps — only real tracker + BetaAdminPanel.

## Forbidden (grounds for ending contract)

- Paid boosts / ads of any kind  
- Purchased reviews, upvotes, or fake waitlist signups  
- Sharing `PRIVATE_ACCESS_SECRET` publicly  
- Pitching medical claims, genomics, or “guaranteed results”

Same rules: organic communities per [SOCIAL_LAUNCH.md](SOCIAL_LAUNCH.md). Creator micro-sponsorships only if founder approves **and** week-1 activation looks real — still not open paid social until week-4 holds.

# Pregnancy, miscarriage, and postpartum

Mission Winning is **educational fitness software**. It is not prenatal care, not a clinician, and not emergency services.

You can optionally set a training status under **Account → More settings**: none, pregnant, postpartum, or miscarriage recovery. It is **never required** to log a set. We do not guess this from your workouts.

## Stopping is allowed

Stop whenever you need to. That is always allowed.

If you have **bleeding, cramping, feel faint, have chest pain, or cannot talk: stop and get help.** Call **local emergency services** — not this app.

## What the app will not do

While that status is set:

- Mission Coach will not prescribe a max-effort session, a field test, or a jump in load.
- Max-test and fitness-test start buttons stay hidden.
- Coach will only say this is **not medical advice; ask your clinician.**

You can still log a normal set. Estimated strength from sets you already logged may still show. That is not a request to test a max.

This app **does not prevent miscarriage** or complications. It does not claim you are medically cleared to train. Pregnancy, miscarriage, and postpartum decisions belong to you and your clinician.

# Estimated 1RM (e1RM)

After you log a working set on Train, the exercise row can show an **estimated one-rep max**.

This is a **formula estimate**, not a tested max. Mission Winning uses the **Epley** formula from the weight and reps you already logged. It is educational — it is not a prescription, not medical advice, and not a “safe PT” number.

## What counts

- Completed working sets on that exercise in this session
- Warm-up sets (W) and zero-load / bodyweight skip sets do not feed the formula
- No account required — guests see it from sets on this device

## Hide it

Open the exercise overflow menu and tap **Hide estimate**. The choice stays on this device. Tap **Show e1RM estimate** to bring it back.

## What this is not

- Not “your max”
- Not a prompt to test a one-rep max
- Not an official military or service test score

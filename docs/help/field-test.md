# Five-event field test

A free logger session on Train. It records five performances. It is not a new tab, not a paid SKU, and not a recruiter.

## The five events

1. **3-rep max deadlift** — one set of three; log the weight.
2. **Hand-release push-up** — count reps in two minutes.
3. **Sprint-drag-carry** — log finish time in seconds. Needs a sled and a 25 m lane.
4. **Plank** — log hold time in seconds.
5. **2-mile run** — road or treadmill; log finish time in seconds.

Timed events use the same convention as plank: seconds live in the reps field.

## How to start

On Train with no session open, tap **Five-event field test** next to Builder. Or open `/active?fieldTest=1`. Finish a field test and tap **Run again** on the receipt.

It does not appear on Today’s Quick options.

## Garage substitute

If you do not have a sled, use **No sled — shuttle + farmer carry** on that event. That stand-in is **unscored (garage)** and drops the optional 0–500 total. You can also skip the event.

The 2-mile on a treadmill is still the official distance and can be scored.

## Points (optional)

Points are off until you pick a **published scale column** on the receipt (age band + column M or F). That is a table column, not an identity check.

With a column set, each official event looks up **0–100** from the published tables. A **0–500** total appears only when all five official events were scored — garage or skip omits the total.

**Bands** on the receipt are Mission Winning labels on those 0–100 rows: Below 60 · Minimum (60–69) · Strong (70–89) · Maximum (90–100).

`72 / 100` and `360 / 500` are point-scale fractions, not a percentile. This app does not rank you against other athletes.

vs last compares this field test to your previous field test only.

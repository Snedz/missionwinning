# Super Bundle vs the four-app stack

Mission Winning sells **one Super Bundle** (~$59/year founders). It is not MyFitnessPal + Pliability + Calm + a coach app glued together.

There is **no trial** and no à la carte pillar checkout. The workout logger stays free.

## What we are not

| They sell | Super Bundle instead |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Strong / Hevy** — paid logger | Logger on `/active` stays **free**. Never gated. |
| **Fitbod / coach chat apps** — chatbot + black-box week | Mission Coach writes a **why-line from your logs**: inputs · rule · effect. It **adapts** when you miss, change gear, or report low readiness. Chat is optional chrome; it is not the SKU. |
| **Pliability** — video mobility shelf | Timed original Move flows. Counts on `/bundle` follow shipped floors, not a trailer number. |
| **MyFitnessPal** — food database / barcode | Fuel is recipes + protein-first coaching from training load. Barcode search may exist as a tool; it is not what you pay for. |
| **Calm** — library + streaks | Mind sessions are short, skippable, and training-adjacent. **No streak guilt.** |

## One SKU

Coach depth plus Fuel, Move, Mind, Track, and Learn when you want them. Exact floors live on `/bundle` and in `CONTENT_FLOORS` — help does not retype them.

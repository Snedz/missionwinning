# Super Bundle vs buying four apps

Mission Winning sells **one Super Bundle** (~$59/year founders). It is not a food diary plus a mobility shelf plus a sit app plus a coach chatbot glued together.

There is **no trial** and no à la carte pillar checkout. The workout logger stays free.

## What we are not

| Category stack | Super Bundle instead |
|----------------|----------------------|
| **Paid logger** | Logger on `/active` stays **free**. Never gated. |
| **Coach chat / black-box week** | Mission Coach writes a **why-line from your logs**: inputs · rule · effect. It **adapts** when you miss, change gear, or report low readiness. Chat is optional chrome; it is not the SKU. |
| **Video mobility shelf** | Timed original Move flows. Counts on `/bundle` follow shipped floors, not a trailer number. |
| **Food database / barcode** | Fuel is recipes + protein-first coaching from training load. Barcode search may exist as a tool; it is not what you pay for. |
| **Sit library + streaks** | Mind sessions are short, skippable, and training-adjacent. **No streak guilt.** |

## One SKU

Coach depth plus Fuel, Move, Mind, Track, and Learn when you want them. Exact floors live on `/bundle` and in `CONTENT_FLOORS` — help does not retype them.

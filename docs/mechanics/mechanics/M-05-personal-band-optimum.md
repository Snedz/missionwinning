**The inversion.** Almost every fitness readout rewards *more*. This one is
calibrated against the athlete's own recovery, so the winning move is sometimes
to rest. That single primitive — `optimum_direction: personal-band` — is the
structural defence against the shame loop that kills habit products, because a
day of doing less can be a good day rather than a failed one.

**Already half-built here, and unusually well.** `load.ts` is Foster's session
RPE times duration — deliberately *one multiplication* rather than an invented
composite that would look scientific while meaning nothing — and it returns
`null` under fourteen days rather than a plausible number. `loadGuard.ts` then
allows the signal to do exactly one thing: **hold** progression. It never
deloads, never cuts sets, never touches session count, on the stated grounds
that declining to add weight on contested evidence is a defensible coaching call
and taking weight away on it is not.

That restraint is the mechanic's real content. The remaining gap is not more
computation; it is that the athlete rarely sees the band, so the product's most
honest number is also its most invisible one.

The axis where this product's constraints bite hardest, and that is a feature of
the design rather than a problem with it. Almost every mechanic the wider
industry uses to drive telling is a comparison mechanic, and comparison is
forbidden inbound by `X-01`.

What survives is one-way projection: a share card built from a closed key set —
call sign, number, kit, table, badges, rank title, tier — with no workout rows,
no free text, and a builder that only accepts picks and derived numbers, so a
`bio` field fails a test.

Left open honestly: nothing here can be graded without users, and the share
event fires on a surface most athletes will not reach before their first
session. A candidate on this axis should expect to be `blocked-on-telemetry`.

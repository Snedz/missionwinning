The wedge's whole claim. The engine already reads training load the way a strap
would without one, re-spreads a missed week, swaps a session on low readiness,
and refuses to bump its own revision number unless sessions actually changed.

So the gap is not capability, it is **legibility and consent**. Today the week
arrives as an assertion. The athlete is told the plan adapted; they are not shown
what changed, why, or given anywhere to disagree. `weekRationale.ts` and
`logCitation.ts` were both built against exactly this — the second one shipped
because survey clarity scored 2.56 out of 5.

`M-01` is the mechanic aimed here, and it comes from a developer tool, which is
the point: the machinery has nothing to do with fitness.

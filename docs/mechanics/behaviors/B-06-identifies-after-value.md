Named `pay` on the archive axis, but the thing actually wanted is not money —
money is muted by a default-true flag until the EIN lands, and no agent may
change that. The scarce asset is **identification**: an account, an email, a
device that can be reached again.

The framing is already written down. The Mission Score is a weekly grade that
resets and points are the odometer, so the odometer is the thing worth keeping,
and *"your history outlives your phone"* is a reason to identify that an athlete
actually wants — **offered after value and never as a wall**. That last clause is
a constraint on every candidate here: a mechanic that front-loads the ask, however
well it converts elsewhere, is proposing a wall.

`M-04` is the mechanic aimed at this, and it is the clearest case in the graph
of a hypothesis that is honest about being ungradeable today.

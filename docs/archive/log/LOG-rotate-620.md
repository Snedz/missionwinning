## 2026-08-08 — The boundary the planner never had (`.605`)

**The finding that made this a contract rather than a guideline.** Kolnes et al. 2026 (*PMC12938745*, n=225 active club runners, mixed-methods) reports that runners who **deleted their own training sessions** because their pace felt slow scored higher on other-avoidance goals. On Strava that costs some vanity: a logged run is a post. Here a logged set is the **planner's input** — [`src/lib/coach/`](src/lib/coach/INDEX.md) builds the week from logs alone, with no wearable to cross-check against — so a session withheld because it looked bad is a lie told to the planner, and the planner then prescribes for an athlete who does not exist. Deloads do not fire; progression runs on a fictional record. Social comparison here is an **input-integrity attack on the core algorithm**, not a matter of tone, and prose cannot fail a build. Research: [DESIGN_RESEARCH.md](docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md) Wave 9. Architecture: [IDENTITY_SOCIAL_PLAN.md](docs/IDENTITY_SOCIAL_PLAN.md).

**This is the repo's first real import-graph guard, and the prior art is why it is not depth-3.** [`launchTruth.test.ts:182`](src/lib/launchTruth.test.ts)'s `reachesRule` is `@/lib/`-only, `.ts`-only, static-only and capped at three hops — it exists to survive one rule moving one file, not to hold a wall.

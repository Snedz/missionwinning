## One-liner / plain language

Adaptive AI training coach for people who train at home or in a park. Free offline workout logging (no account). Weekly plans adapt from logs alone — no wearable required.

## Why massively scalable?

Billions of smartphones; gyms and WHOOP-class sensors are scarce. Wearable-first coaches structurally miss the train-anywhere majority. A free PWA + Coach that runs on logs alone can distribute without app-store tax and without sensor ownership. Beachhead English communities → LATAM/SEA via PWA.

## Product / technology

Shipped Mission Coach engine (generate week, adapt on miss/readiness, adjust today, optional LLM voice/chat with ZDR). Client-side deterministic core + premium depth. Free logger is the wedge; Super Bundle is the revenue layer.

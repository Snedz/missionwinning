## Article 1 — Formation

1.1 The Company is formed as a limited liability company under the laws of `[STATE]` under the name **Mission Winning LLC** (the “Company”).

1.2 The Company continues until dissolved under this Agreement or applicable law.

1.3 The registered agent is `[REGISTERED_AGENT]`. The principal office is `[PRINCIPAL_OFFICE]`.

## Article 2 — Purpose

2.1 The Company’s purpose is to develop, host, and monetize educational fitness software and related services under the Mission Winning brand (including the web app / PWA, Super Bundle subscriptions, and related content), and any lawful activity incidental thereto.

2.2 The Company does **not** provide medical care, medical devices, or licensed professional certification.

## Article 3 — Members and management

3.1 The Company is a **single-member** LLC. The sole Member is `[MEMBER_LEGAL_NAME]`.

3.2 The Company is **member-managed**. The Member has full authority to manage the business, open bank and payment accounts (including Stripe), enter contracts, hire contractors, and bind the Company.

3.3 No additional members may be admitted except by written amendment signed by the Member (and counsel as needed).

## Article 4 — Capital and distributions

4.1 Initial capital contributions: as recorded in the Company’s books (cash, IP assignment, or other property).

4.2 Additional contributions are voluntary unless the Member agrees otherwise in writing.

4.3 Distributions of available cash may be made to the Member at such times as the Member determines, after reserving amounts for taxes, known liabilities, and operating needs (including hosting and payment processor reserves).

## Article 5 — Banking and payments authority

5.1 The Member may open and maintain deposit accounts and payment processor accounts (Stripe, PayPal, crypto rails as applicable) in the Company’s name.

5.2 The Member is authorized to designate payout bank accounts and to resolve refunds and chargebacks in the ordinary course, consistent with Company policies (including the public Refunds policy at missionwinning.com/refunds).

## Article 6 — Intellectual property

6.1 The Member hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Mission Winning software, documentation, trademarks, domain names, and related goodwill developed for the business, to the extent owned by the Member, effective as of `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]` (or a separate short-form IP assignment of even date).

6.2 Work product created by contractors for the Company shall be owned by the Company under written work-for-hire / assignment agreements whenever practicable.

## Article 7 — Liability and indemnification

7.1 Except as required by law, the Member’s liability for Company obligations is limited as provided under `[STATE]` LLC law.

7.2 The Company shall indemnify the Member against claims arising from good-faith acts on behalf of the Company, to the fullest extent permitted by law, excluding fraud, willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law.

## Article 8 — Records and tax

8.1 The Company shall keep books and records adequate for tax and bank KYC.

8.2 Default tax classification for a single-member LLC is typically disregarded entity (schedule C) unless the Member elects otherwise — **confirm with a CPA**.

## Article 9 — Dissolution

9.1 The Company dissolves upon the Member’s written election, or as required by law.

9.2 On dissolution, after paying or reserving for liabilities, remaining assets distribute to the Member.

## Article 10 — Governing law; amendments

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of `[STATE]`, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

10.2 Amendments must be in a writing signed by the Member.

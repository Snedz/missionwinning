# Acceptable Use Policy — Mission Winning

**Live page:** [missionwinning.com/usage](https://missionwinning.com/usage) · **Supported Regions:** [/regions](https://missionwinning.com/regions) (**Europe is not supported**).

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) applies to the Mission Winning application, APIs, hosted services, and any use of the open-source codebase (the “Software”). By using the Software or services, you agree to these rules in addition to the [Terms of Service](https://missionwinning.com/terms).

**Purpose:** Keep the free training mission safe in Supported Regions. Open source builds trust; it does not license abuse.

---

## 1. Permitted use

You may use Mission Winning to:

- Track workouts, nutrition, mobility, mind, and learning for yourself or people you coach with their consent
- Run a self-hosted or modified copy under the [AGPL-3.0](../../LICENSE) license
- Contribute improvements that respect privacy, safety, and the free-core mission

---

## 2. Prohibited use

You must **not** use the Software, hosted services, generated content, or any derived service to:

### 2.1 Illegal deepfakes and non-consensual synthetic media

- Create, distribute, or monetize **illegal deepfakes**, including non-consensual intimate imagery or deceptive synthetic media of real people
- Generate or host sexualized synthetic media of minors (any depiction — see also CSAM below)
- Use AI features (if enabled) to impersonate a real person for fraud, harassment, or political deception where prohibited by law

### 2.2 Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)

- Create, possess, upload, request, or distribute any child sexual abuse material or any sexual content involving minors (real or fictional where illegal)
- We will report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authorities and providers as required by law

### 2.3 Fraud, scams, and financial crime

- Phishing, identity theft, payment fraud, crypto scams, or social-engineering attacks
- Abuse of checkout, coupons, school PINs, or account systems to steal access or money
- Money laundering or other financial crimes

### 2.4 Health and safety abuse

- Present Mission Winning as medical diagnosis, emergency care, or licensed clinical advice
- Promote dangerous self-harm, disordered-eating coercion, or illegal performance-enhancing distribution schemes
- Use school/youth features to exploit or endanger minors

### 2.5 Security and infrastructure abuse

- Unauthorized access, scanning, or exploitation of systems you do not own
- DDoS, malware distribution, credential stuffing, or bypassing rate limits and auth
- Scraping premium content at scale in violation of terms or robots rules meant to protect the free core

### 2.6 Harassment and illegal content

- Threats, stalking, doxxing, or targeted harassment
- Content that is illegal in your jurisdiction (hate crimes, trafficking facilitation, etc.)

## 3. Youth and school features

Class codes, parental consent flows, and Presidential Fitness–style tools are for legitimate education and coaching only. Do not collect youth data without required consent. Do not use youth features for advertising profiles or sale of children’s data.

---

## 4. Self-hosted and modified copies

If you run a fork or self-hosted instance:

- You remain responsible for lawful operation and for honoring AGPL-3.0 (including source disclosure for network use)
- You must not strip this AUP or license notices to enable prohibited use
- Hosted Mission Winning operators may refuse to interconnect with instances used for prohibited purposes

---

## 5. Enforcement

Mission Winning LLC may:

- Suspend or terminate accounts that violate this AUP
- Remove content, revoke API access, or block abusive IPs
- Preserve and share information with law enforcement when legally required
- Refuse support for deployments used primarily for prohibited activities

Nothing in this AUP limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

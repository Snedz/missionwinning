You represent that you are not a person or entity designated on any applicable restricted-party, sanctions, or denied-persons list maintained by authorities in jurisdictions where we or our payment, cloud, or distribution partners operate, and that you will not use the Service in any way that would cause Mission Winning LLC or those partners to violate applicable sanctions, export-control, or trade-control laws. We may refuse, suspend, or terminate access where we reasonably believe applicable law forbids providing the Service — including comprehensively restricted jurisdictions and other persons or destinations restricted under applicable sanctions or trade-control programs — even when automated country detection is imperfect. Our commercial Supported Regions exclusions (including Ukraine as a whole) are product-availability rules and are not a claim about any single country’s embargo list. You remain responsible for complying with laws that apply to you.

### Regions summary
Mission Winning’s hosted consumer service (website, PWA accounts, cloud sync, Super Bundle checkout, and our distributed Android product) is a global product with listed commercial exclusions: Europe (including France), Canada, Ukraine, and Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) member states. Cloudflare edge rules plus in-app signup/checkout hard blocks enforce these exclusions. Ukraine is a commercial product exclusion, separate from sanctions and trade-control compliance.

### Regions supported
We offer the hosted service globally outside the excluded territories, wherever we choose to operate and where payment and infrastructure partners allow. Availability of payments, app stores, or specific features may still vary by country inside Supported Regions.

### Regions not supported
We do not support: (1) Europe — EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland, France, and associated territories; (2) Canada; (3) Ukraine; (4) all 57 Organisation of Islamic Cooperation member states. Do not create a hosted account, purchase Super Bundle, or expect customer support if you reside there. Signup and checkout are hard-blocked in-app when your CDN country matches these lists (in addition to Cloudflare). We do not appoint an EU/UK GDPR representative for this consumer offering. Separately, applicable sanctions, export-control, and trade-control laws in jurisdictions where we and our partners operate may forbid providing the Service in comprehensively restricted jurisdictions and to other persons or destinations restricted under those programs. We may refuse, suspend, or terminate access for legal compliance even when automated country detection is imperfect. See Terms — Sanctions, export controls & prohibited persons.

### Usage prohibited (territory/sanctions)
Using the hosted Service from an unsupported region in violation of Supported Regions, or using the Service in a way that would cause Mission Winning LLC or its partners to violate applicable sanctions, export-control, or trade-control laws (including comprehensively restricted jurisdictions and other persons or destinations restricted under those programs).

### Service regions
All service lines above that depend on Mission Winning LLC hosting, accounts, or payments are limited to Supported Regions. Europe, Canada, Ukraine, and OIC member states are not supported. See /regions.

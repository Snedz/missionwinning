# Entity research — Mission Winning LLC

**Not legal/tax advice.** Research memo for a US founder shipping Mission Winning (educational fitness PWA / SaaS). Confirm with a CPA and formation attorney in your jurisdiction before filing.

## Recommendation (default)

**Form a single-member LLC in the founder’s home state** unless counsel or future investors specifically require Delaware.

| Why home-state first | Why not rush Delaware / Wyoming |
|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Lower ongoing franchise tax / reporting for many states | Delaware annual franchise tax + registered agent forever |
| One set of filings if you live and bank there | Selling nationwide still often requires **foreign qualification** in other states later |
| Simple member-managed OA (see draft) | Wyoming “anonymity” is often oversold; banks/KYC still need a real person |
| Enough for Stripe under the LLC + Super Bundle | DE shines more for multi-member / VC preferred stock later |

Revisit Delaware if you raise institutional capital or add co-founders who insist on DE corporate norms.

## Comparison snapshot

| Factor | Home-state LLC | Delaware LLC | Wyoming LLC |
|--------|----------------|--------------|-------------|
| Formation | State portal / service | Corp service + DE agent | Corp service + WY agent |
| Ongoing cost | Varies (often modest) | Franchise tax + agent | Agent + annual report |
| Privacy | Public member info varies by state | Manager/member disclosure rules apply | Often marketed as private — still not a substitute for counsel |
| Nationwide sales | Foreign qualify when nexus requires | Same | Same |
| Investor familiarity | Fine for bootstrapped SaaS | Preferred for many VC deals | Less common for SaaS raises |

**Registered agent:** Required in formation state (and each foreign-qualified state). Use a commercial agent if you move or travel.

**EIN:** Get free from IRS after formation (or SS-4). Needed for business bank + Stripe.

**Business bank:** Open under LLC name (Mercury, Relay, or local). Do not mix personal rent with Stripe payouts.

**IP assignment:** Assign Mission Winning app IP, brand, and domain registrations **into the LLC** (OA § IP + short IP assignment). Avoid leaving core IP only in a personal name once charging customers.

**Terms stamp:** After formation, replace “state of formation of Mission Winning LLC” in `/terms` with the **actual** state (e.g. “State of Texas”). See [LEGAL_SAFETY.md](../LEGAL_SAFETY.md).

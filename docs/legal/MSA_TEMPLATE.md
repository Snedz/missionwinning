**Status:** Template for customization with an Order Form. Consumer app remains best-effort hosted software under [Terms](https://missionwinning.com/terms).

## 1. Parties and structure

- **Provider:** Mission Winning LLC
- **Customer:** [legal name]
- This MSA + Order Form(s) + [DPA](DPA.md) (if processing personal data on Customer’s behalf) form the agreement
- Order Form controls fees, seats, term, and any special SLAs

## 2. Services

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use Mission Winning (hosted PWA / agreed modules) for Customer’s internal business or educational purposes during the Subscription Term.

**Educational fitness software only** — not a medical device; not HIPAA-certified healthcare; not a substitute for professional medical advice.

## 3. Fees and payment

As stated on the Order Form. Late fees / suspension after [X] days past due by written notice. Taxes are Customer’s responsibility unless Provider is legally required to collect.

## 4. Support (default — override in Order Form)

| Priority | Meaning | Target acknowledgment |
|----------|---------|------------------------|
| P1 | Service fully unavailable for Customer’s production use | Within **1 business day** via support@missionwinning.com |
| P2 | Major feature impaired | Within **2 business days** |
| P3 | General questions | Best effort |

**Uptime:** Best-effort availability using Provider’s cloud hosts (currently Vercel + Supabase). **No public consumer SLA** and **no 99.9% guarantee** in this template unless an Order Form explicitly adds measured uptime with credits. Scheduled maintenance excluded.

## 5. Customer responsibilities

- Lawful use; Acceptable Use / Terms where applicable
- Accurate admin contacts; secure credentials
- Configuration of student/parent consent where required (COPPA / local youth law)
- Content and instructions for any data Processor role under the DPA

## 6. Intellectual property

Provider retains all rights in the Service and Marks. Customer retains rights in Customer Data. Feedback may be used by Provider without obligation.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other’s Confidential Information with reasonable care for [3] years (trade secrets longer).

## 8. Data protection

If Provider processes Personal Data on Customer’s behalf, the [DPA](DPA.md) applies. Consumer end-users of the public app remain under Provider’s Privacy Policy.

## 9. Warranties and disclaimer

Provider warrants it will provide the Service in a professional manner. **Except as stated, the Service is provided “as is.”** No warranty of uninterrupted or error-free operation, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement beyond applicable law.

## 10. Indemnity (high-level)

- Provider: third-party IP claims alleging the Service as provided infringes, subject to exclusions (Customer mods, combo with third-party, etc.)
- Customer: claims arising from Customer Data, unlawful instructions, or misuse

## 11. Liability cap

Except for [willful misconduct / unpaid fees / confidentiality / IP indemnity — tune with counsel], each party’s aggregate liability under the Agreement is limited to **fees paid by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12) months** before the claim. No indirect, consequential, or lost-profits damages to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 12. Insurance (placeholder)

Upon written request after [policy obtained], Provider will maintain commercially reasonable **cyber liability** insurance and provide a certificate. Until obtained, this section is aspirational — see founder checklist in [LEGAL_SAFETY.md](../LEGAL_SAFETY.md).

## 13. Term and termination

Initial term and renewals per Order Form. Either party may terminate for material breach uncured [30] days after notice. On termination, Customer access ends; data return/deletion per DPA.

## 14. Governing law

Laws of the United States and the **state of formation of Mission Winning LLC** (confirm in Order Form), excluding conflict-of-law rules. Venue: courts / arbitration as agreed in Order Form (consumer AAA Terms do not automatically bind B2B deals).

## 15. General

Entire agreement; amendments in writing; assignment with consent (except affiliate / change of control with notice); severability; notices to emails on Order Form.

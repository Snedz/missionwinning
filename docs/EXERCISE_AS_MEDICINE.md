# Exercise as medicine — evidence thesis

**Audience:** Founder, agents writing brand/social/YC/About copy  
**Constitution:** [vision.md](../vision.md) · **Wedge (unchanged):** [YC_THESIS.md](YC_THESIS.md) · **Claim rules:** [brand-guidelines.md](brand-guidelines.md) · [LEGAL_SAFETY.md](LEGAL_SAFETY.md)

This file is the **source of truth for the 92% / SSRI framing**. Do not invent stats elsewhere — cite here or omit.

---

## One paragraph

Exercise has strong clinical-trial support for reducing **mild to moderate** depressive symptoms — often comparable to antidepressants in research settings. Guidelines already name exercise as a first-line option for mild depression. Yet most mental health professionals never received formal training in **exercise prescription**, so advice collapses to “just go work out” — no dose, no progression, no plan when life happens. Mission Winning does **not** treat depression. We build the missing layer: **structured, adaptive training people can actually follow** on any phone (free logger + Mission Coach).

## Product implication

| Medicine gets | Vague advice | Mission Winning |
|---------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Drug, dose, duration | “Go work out” | Weekly sessions, intensity intent, progression |
| Adjustment when side effects / life change | Guilt or abandon | Fatigue-aware adapt / lighter week / Just Go |
| Follow-up | Nothing | Logs → Coach revisions → week-4 habit |

**Wedge stays Train + Mission Coach** for train-anywhere athletes. Mood/energy language is an honest secondary outcome of showing up — not a clinical product, not a Calm clone, not a depression SEO farm.

**Boundary (locked):** Educational fitness tools and a daily supplement — not medical care, not a replacement for clinicians, therapists, or medication decisions.

## Citations (use these)

| Claim | Source | Accurate phrasing |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| Exercise reduces depressive symptoms; many modalities show moderate effects vs active controls | [BMJ 2024 network meta-analysis](https://www.bmj.com/content/384/bmj-2023-075847) (218 studies) | “Hundreds of trials support exercise for depressive symptoms…” |
| Exercise ≈ antidepressants / combination for **non-severe** depression (no clear difference in NMA) | [BJSM comparative NMA](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2022-105964) | “…often comparable to medication in research settings for mild / non-severe depression.” |
| **92%** of surveyed MHPs reported **no formal training** in exercise prescription | [PMC7236166](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7236166/) (Brazilian MHP sample) | “~92% of **mental health professionals** in that survey…” — **not** “92% of doctors” unless you cite a doctors-only study |
| Popular synthesis of the gap (guidelines vs practice) | [Big Think](https://bigthink.com/mind-behavior/exercise-works-for-depression-so-why-isnt-it-treated-like-real-medicine/) · [MindSite News](https://mindsitenews.org/2026/07/22/exercise-works-for-depression-almost-nobody-prescribes-it/) | OK for founder narrative; still pair with primary papers above |

Always add: *Mission Winning is not a medical provider; this is not advice to stop or change prescribed treatment.*

## Allowed / forbidden claims

### Allowed

- “Exercise is medicine” as a **thesis** (structured dose > vague advice).
- Energy, mood, resilience, stress as **outcomes of consistent training**.
- “Hundreds of trials…” / “mild to moderate…” / “comparable in research settings…” with citation context.
- “Most mental health clinicians never got formal training in prescribing exercise — so advice stays vague.”
- “We turn training into a clear, adaptive weekly plan you can follow on any phone.”

### Forbidden

- “We treat / cure / diagnose depression” (or any mental illness).
- “Replace your SSRI / therapist / psychiatrist.”
- “As good as SSRIs for everyone / for severe depression.”
- Leading landing hero, Product Hunt, or paid ads with clinical depression.
- “92% of **doctors**” without a matching citation (use **mental health professionals** + survey context).
- Crisis features framed as clinical care; self-harm or disordered-eating coercion ([ACCEPTABLE_USE.md](legal/ACCEPTABLE_USE.md)).

### Layer rule

| Layer | Depression / exercise-medicine language? |
|-------|------------------------------------------|
| North star ([vision.md](../vision.md)) | Yes — why the body matters |
| Wedge / landing hero | No clinical lead; optional energy/mood below fold |
| About / Learn / founder social | Yes — educational + disclaimer |
| Product UX (Coach, victory) | Feel/energy check-ins — never diagnosis |

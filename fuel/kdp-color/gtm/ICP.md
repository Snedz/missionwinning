# Open Plate — ICP

One product, one buyer. This is not a growth memo for Mission Winning and not a war-room file.

---

## Who this is for

**First-time KDP publishers** who want a **color-interior coloring paperback** and are stuck on the print file, not on “how to make money online.”

They usually:

- Have already opened KDP and seen the 24-page minimum
- Tried a black-and-white template and then switched to color without changing bleed or paper
- Do not want licensed characters
- Will sit with a print dialog and a ruler

The job we help: **a spec-correct interior they can proof locally**, plus listing fields that do not lie.

## Who this is not for

- People buying a “KDP cash” or rank-claim course
- Farms that stamp the same mandala 24 times
- Anyone who needs a cover, ads, or a done-for-you Amazon account
- Mission Winning athletes (wrong product)
- resume-kit or invoice-lite buyers (parallel SKUs, different job)

## Buying moment

They search or ask: “KDP color interior bleed 8.5 x 11 coloring book page count.”

The channel that matches that moment is the **KDP Help Community** (see `OUTREACH_DRAFTS.md`). Amazon search via the listing is the storefront **after** upload — there is no listing URL today.

## Offer they can evaluate

- 12 unique plates they can open as SVG
- A 24-page HTML assembly they can print to PDF
- Export notes that name trim, bleed, safe, and the color-ink choice
- A listing draft stamped UNPUBLISHED

They cannot evaluate a price, a rank, or a checkout. Those do not exist yet.

## Language that stays out

No get-rich pitch, “low content goldmine,” review counts, or “we sold n copies.” If a sentence needs a number we did not measure, delete the sentence.

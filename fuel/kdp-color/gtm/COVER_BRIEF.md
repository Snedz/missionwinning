# Open Plate — cover brief

**Not a cover file.** Do not upload this page to KDP. The interior pack has no wrap PDF and no barcode.

KDP’s live cover calculator wins if a number here disagrees with the form on upload day.

---

## Intended wrap

- Binding: paperback
- Trim: 8.5 in × 11 in
- Page count for spine math: **24** (the interior assembly)
- Interior type: color (standard vs premium — founder picks after print-cost preview)
- Paper: white

Spine on 24 color pages is thin. Do not put a long title on the spine. A small word mark or a blank spine is honest.

## Front

- Title: Open Plate
- One still of **line art**, not a colored-in sample that looks like a finished painting
- No review stars, no rank badge, no “look inside” claim
- Keep type 0.25 in inside trim; full-bleed color field is allowed if it reaches the bleed edge

## Back

- Short honest blurb: twelve original plates, 8.5 × 11, color interior
- Leave the barcode box empty in any draft. KDP drops the barcode. Do not invent an ISBN to fill it
- No QR code to a checkout. `paymentUrl` is empty

## Refuse on the wrap

- Licensed characters
- A photo of a rank banner or a store display of other people’s books
- Mission Winning marks (wrong product)
- A second SKU title (Mission Galaxy is a stub, not a series yet)

Put the PDF next to this brief and write the real filename here. Until then this folder has interiors only.

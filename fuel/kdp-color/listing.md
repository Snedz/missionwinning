## Description (plain text, no HTML claims)

Open Plate is a coloring book interior built for print.

The book has twenty-four pages: title and copyright, a short how-to, twelve original black-line plates, six notes and palette-test pages, and closing matter. Plates are not copied to pad the page count.

Subjects: nested geometry, vines and leaves, window grids, waves, star tilings, radial botanicals, block plans, a flower wheel, ridgelines, interlace, circle packs, and a fern unfurl. Lines are closed so regions can take color.

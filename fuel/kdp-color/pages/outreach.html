Draft 1 — bleed rejection

Thread shape: color interior PDF rejected for bleed.

DO NOT SEND

Color interiors get rejected when line art or a border sits in the 0.125 in bleed
or the 0.25 in safe band inside trim.

For an 8.5 × 11 paperback I use:

- media (if you bleed): 8.75 × 11.25
- trim: 8.5 × 11
- keep strokes ≥ 0.25 in inside trim

I keep a local 24-page assembly (12 unique plates, no repeated art) and print
to PDF from HTML rather than screenshotting plates. I have not uploaded a live
book from that file yet — treat this as a spec note, not a listing.

If KDP’s form shows a different bleed number on the day you upload, use the form.

Draft 2 — padding to 24 pages

Thread shape: do all 24 pages have to be unique?

DO NOT SEND

KDP’s minimum is 24 pages, not 24 unique drawings. Duplicating one plate to
hit the count looks like a pad and reads badly in the Look Inside preview.

A split that has worked on the desk (not a live ASIN):

- 3 front matter
- 12 unique plates
- 6 notes / palette-test pages that a reader can actually use
- 3 closing

That is 24 without cloning art. I would not call it a published book until
the PDF and cover are through KDP review.

Draft 3 — standard vs premium color

Thread shape: should I pick premium color for a coloring book?

DO NOT SEND

A coloring book interior is still mostly black line on white. Color ink
matters because the interior type is color, not because the plates are
already filled.

I would open the KDP print-cost preview with the same 24-page PDF on
standard color and on premium color, then pick from the cost — not from
a blog that says premium always looks richer.

I do not have a live list price or sales number to quote. If someone
posts one, ask whether it is their own dashboard or a screenshot from
a course.

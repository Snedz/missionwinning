# Invoice Lite — one-channel outreach drafts

**Channel:** public Q&A threads where someone asks how to send a simple invoice.

**DO NOT SEND.** Founder reads, edits, and posts — or does not. Agents do not create accounts, do not reply, do not DM.

No second channel in this pack. No email list. No cross-post.

Each draft answers a layout question. None include a checkout URL. `paymentUrl` is empty. Price is UNKNOWN — do not invent one.

---

## Draft 1 — one page vs a suite

**Thread shape:** “Do I need invoicing software for one freelance job?”

**DO NOT SEND**

```
One job needs one page: who you are, who they are, what you did, a total,
and a date. A suite is for recurring ledgers.

I keep a local one-page HTML I print to PDF. It is not a live product and
there is no store URL. Treat this as a layout note, not a listing.
```

## Draft 2 — paid vs unknown

**Thread shape:** “Should the template say PAID by default?”

**DO NOT SEND**

```
Leave paid as UNKNOWN until you have a receipt. Stamping PAID to look
finished is how a copy and the bank disagree.

I have a local file that defaults to UNKNOWN. I have not charged anyone
for the file. If someone quotes a collection rate, ask whether it is
their own number.
```

## Draft 3 — price without a store

**Thread shape:** “What should a simple invoice template cost?”

**DO NOT SEND**

```
I do not have a list price for the file I use locally. Price is UNKNOWN
until a founder sets one. I have not wired a checkout. A cart without
a payment URL is a prop, not a sale.

Do not copy another SKU’s number onto this one to look finished.
```

Crypto is a **payment rail** (Lifetime USDC), not the product ([docs/CRYPTO_RAILS_THESIS.md](docs/CRYPTO_RAILS_THESIS.md)).

Every correlation product on the market (WHOOP, Oura, Bevel) resolves behaviors to **HRV or a recovery score**; no lifter's coach has ever asked what their HRV did.

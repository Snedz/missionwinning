# Mission Winning - Vision & Product Direction

## Project Overview
**Mission Winning** is an AI-powered fitness & health coaching web app (PWA) that helps users train smarter, recover better, and build lifelong strength and consistency.

We are evolving the app to be **heavily inspired by Bevel Health** — a premium, data-driven AI health coach — while staying true to our mission of accessibility and global impact.

## Bevel-Inspired Direction
Bevel excels at turning wearables + lifestyle data into clear, actionable insights. We will adopt this philosophy:

- Clean, metric-first dashboard (Readiness, Strain, Recovery scores)
- Strong AI coaching with personalized recommendations
- Excellent strength training tools (700+ exercise feel, smart logging, progression)
- Holistic view: training + recovery + habits
- Premium, motivating UI/UX

### Core Pillars (Bevel + Mission Winning)
1. **Dashboard** — Central hub with scores, AI insights, quick actions
2. **AI Coach / Workout Generator** — Personalized plans based on goals, schedule, fatigue
3. **Strength Builder** — Robust exercise library, set/reps/weight logger, real-time feedback
4. **Progress Tracking** — Volume, strength progression, charts, streaks
5. **Journal / Habits** — Daily logging, mood, recovery notes
6. **Future** — Nutrition, sleep, wearable integration

## Key Goals
- Feel premium and intelligent like Bevel
- Excellent for serious lifters (strength focus)
- Fast workout logging
- Smart AI that adapts to user progress and feedback
- Freemium model: Strong free core + Pro for advanced AI/features

## Tech Stack
- Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript
- Tailwind + shadcn/ui (modern, clean design)
- Supabase (auth, DB, storage)
- AI integration (Grok, Claude, etc.)
- PWA ready
- Vercel deployment

## Success Criteria
- Users describe it as “Bevel but more gym/strength focused and accessible”
- High engagement on logging and AI plans
- Clear progression tracking that motivates consistency
- Scalable to revenue via Stripe Pro subscriptions

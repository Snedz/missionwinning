## Lander blurbs

### Hero

**ClearShot (working title)**  
Share the screenshot. Not the account number.

### Subhead

On-device redact and blur. Opens from the iOS share sheet. No upload. No account.

### What it does (when built)

- Detect text on the shot (Apple Vision, on-device)  
- One-tap hide: emails, phone numbers, card-shaped digits  
- Brush blur / pixel for faces and anything Vision misses  
- Crop, arrow, and a single highlight  
- Export back to the share sheet

### What it does not do

- Cloud “AI privacy”  
- Device mockups and gradient marketing shots (see screenshot-stack-pages)  
- Live in Mission Winning today (the MW mini is an empty stub)

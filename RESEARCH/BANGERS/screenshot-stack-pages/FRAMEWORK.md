# screenshot-stack-pages — framework

**Claim:** Indie app makers will pay for **store-ready screenshot stacks** (5–8 framed pages) faster than they will pay for a new generator SaaS.

**Status:** paper. **Pays:** 0. **Checkout:** none.

## Problem

App Store / Play listings need a **stack** of screenshots with type, frames, and locales. Generators already charge ~$19–49/mo or one-time kits. The painful job is often **one app, one week**, not a subscription.

## Buyer

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Who | Solo iOS/Android devs shipping a listing this month |
| Who is not | Designers who want a redact-first cleaner (that is [clearshot-ios](../clearshot-ios/)) |
| Job | Five to eight store-sized framed pages, type included |
| Willingness | Category already paid. Our $39 stack vs $19 templates is **HYPOTHESIS**. |

## Wedge

**Sell the stack, not the studio.** A $29–49 one-shot pack (Figma or PNG) for *their* app, or a tiny template pack, **before** building AppLaunchpad.

**Cleaner vs beautifier:** [clearshot-ios](../clearshot-ios/) is **redact-first** (share-sheet privacy). This slug is **store mockups / stacks**. Keep three offers with [unlock-code-utils](../unlock-code-utils/) (copy codes). Do not ship one “screenshot megapp” lander. Complementary rule: [OVERNIGHT_GLM53.md](../OVERNIGHT_GLM53.md).

## MW relationship

None. Do not generate MW listing art from the athlete app. Do not scrape competitor templates. Separate checkout from Super Bundle.

## 14-day test

See [SELL_FIRST.md](SELL_FIRST.md). Bar: **5 cold pays / 14 days**. Rank 2 — second-easiest after ClearShot. Founder picks **one** offer: custom $39 **or** template $19. Do not run the same 14 days as ClearShot.

## Standing rules

1. **Sell before build.** A landing + waitlist + payment intent is the product until the kill bar is beaten.
2. **`PAYMENT_URL` is a placeholder** until the founder pastes a real checkout. Agents never invent Stripe/Apple links or traction.
3. **Kill is a number.** Soft qualitative “interest” does not save a track.
4. **MW iOS stays deferred** ([docs/IOS_PLAYBOOK.md](../../docs/IOS_PLAYBOOK.md)). A BANGERS iOS bet is not the MW iOS lane.
5. One concern per PR. Do not merge a BANGERS pack into a Train/Coach ship.

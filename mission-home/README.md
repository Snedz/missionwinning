## What this is

A loft with industrial bones and residential warmth. Six rooms below are the brief: coffee fuel wall, spiral stair, island, library, shoe closet, sliding wardrobe. Each room steals a lived-in pattern and skips the photo-first version that adds housework or pretends to be a store.

The house fits the people who actually walk it. That is 适我主义. It is not a second style catalog and not a Mission Winning product surface.

## Coffee fuel wall

**Steal.** A vertical daily-ritual wall at the kitchen edge — kettle, beans, cups, one cloth — inside a single reach of the island. In this house “fuel” means morning coffee, not a cafe brand. Closed uppers or a sliding plane so the wall can go quiet when the ritual is done. Keep the field warm (see [MATERIALS.md](MATERIALS.md) 2700K). Pair with 早C on the island, not a second station across the loft.

**Skip.** Neon cafe lettering. Logo walls. Open spice theater that becomes weekly dusting. Merchandising the product. A wall that photographs as a shop.

## Spiral stair

**Steal.** Compact footprint between loft levels. A real handrail. Tread lights in the same warm kelvin as the field so a night descent is not a cool-white gallery. If a service stair already exists, keep the spiral as the light path and the service stair as the grocery / furniture path.

**Skip.** Glass with no rail (photo stair). Using the spiral as the only load path when furniture will not turn. Claiming a calendar finish you have not measured. Painting it as a sculpture that fights the hand.

## Island

**Steal.** 早C岛台 — the morning start station. One seating side. Outlets for the kettle on the fuel-wall side. A top that may mark (soapstone or oak). Circulation around it if the plan allows a loop; otherwise a peninsula that does not block the entry dirt-lock. The island is where the day begins, not a second unused sink.

**Skip.** A show sink that never sees a glass. Waterfall “marble” film. Island as display furniture in the middle of the only walk. Open shelves on the guest face that become 容乱 in the visual field.

## Library

**Steal.** One vertical book plane, warm light, a chair that is actually sat in. This is Learn in the loft: paper you open. Continuity with oak millwork so it reads as architecture, not a purchased “library kit.”

**Skip.** Color-sorted fake spines. Glass shelves that need a weekly cloth. A styled-empty wall for photographs. Turning the plane into a shop window.

## Shoe closet

**Steal.** Entry dirt-lock — the 去家务化 move. Shoes, bag, coat land here. Hands wash before the living field. Closed, ventilated, not visible from the island. A bench if people actually sit to take shoes off.

**Skip.** Open shoe stadium in the foyer. Dropping the day’s bag on the island. A pretty rack that photographs well and tracks grit across oak.

## Sliding wardrobe

**Steal.** A flush plane that *is* the clothes 容乱区. One motion hides the mess so the bedroom or loft wall can stay calm. Oak or a limewash-matched paint so the plane is architecture. Full-height so the top does not become a second dust shelf.

**Skip.** A mirrored wall that doubles visual noise. Boutique lighting and a “walk-in set” that is housework. Leaving the plane open as a display.
